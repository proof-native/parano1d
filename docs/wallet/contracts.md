# Contracts

Use **F7 Contracts** to create a policy, fund it and interact with saved or
received terms. Settings are on F8.

- **Create:** choose a refundable payment, vault, allowance wallet, period
  budget, recurring payment, gradual unlock or custom program. Saving terms
  is free; Call fee limit is the ceiling for later calls.
- **My contracts:** select a saved contract above Actions, Operations & receipts
  and Rules. Check live balances, review a call and track confirmation.
- **Open file:** preview a received contract or call receipt, verify it, then
  add it or merge an update. Existing names and records are preserved.

The journal includes retained calls of both parties. Offline gaps after pruning
may require a receipt or updated terms from the other participant. Current
balance comes from State; a receipt is evidence of a past call.
